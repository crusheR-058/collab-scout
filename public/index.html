    What a scout does, in order

    1. Pins down who you mean

      Matches your brand, or the well-known names it resembles, to Qloo entities and asks you when a match is unclear.

      qloo_describe qloo_find_tags

    2. Maps your audience's taste

      Finds what the same audience likes in music, fashion, food, film and places.

      qloo_recommend qloo_entity_tags

    3. Ranks a shortlist

      Scores every candidate partner together against your audience, so the order means something.

      qloo_rank qloo_compare_audiences

    4. Checks momentum and place

      Looks at who is rising and where the overlap is strongest.

      qloo_trends qloo_where_popular

    5. Writes the brief

      Ranked partners, the Qloo findings behind each one, a pitch you can send, and what the data does not show.
