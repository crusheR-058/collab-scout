What a scout does, in order

  1. Pins down who you mean

    Matches your brand, or the names your audience loves, to Qloo entities. When a name could mean two things, it asks you.

  2. Scans across categories

    Music, brands, podcasts, venues and shows in one pass, each candidate scored with the same published method.

  3. Ranks and checks

    Scores the shortlist together so the numbers compare, then checks momentum and where the overlap is strongest.

  4. Plans the partnership

    A concept, what each side gets, the risks, the questions to ask first and a small test to run before you commit.

What you can check

  • Where each number came fromEvery score is Qloo's, tied to the call that returned it.
  • What was guessedNames Qloo never returned are flagged, not hidden.
  • How the ranking worksA published method. Switch the size preference and see what moves.

What it won't pretend to know

  • Availability and feesQloo knows taste, not calendars or rates.
  • SalesAn overlap is a pattern across many people, not a forecast.
  • Any one personEverything is aggregate. No personal data goes to Qloo.
